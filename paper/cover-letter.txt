Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Exact Large-Neighbourhood Search for
the Maximum Independent Set Problem on Massive Sparse Graphs", for publication
in the European Journal of Operational Research. It is a computational paper on
a core discrete optimisation problem and fits the area of discrete optimisation
and heuristics.

WHAT THE PAPER DOES

The maximum independent set problem (equivalently minimum vertex cover) models
the selection of a largest mutually compatible set. On large sparse graphs the
standard approach applies safe reduction rules and then searches what remains.
The paper studies a region move for that search: release a set of incumbent
vertices, collect the vertices that become eligible, and re-solve the resulting
region by branch-and-reduce. Nothing outside the region conflicts with it, so
the incumbent never worsens.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT NEW

The region construction is that of Borowitz, Grossmann and Schulz (ALENEX 2025)
for dynamic independent sets, and the manuscript says so. Its contribution is
the use of that construction as the inner move of a static solver, with
reductions applied inside each region and an online rule for the number of
released vertices, together with an empirical account that includes negative
results. Against six solvers from 2012-2019 on 27 instances the method is
strictly ahead on three Delaunay graphs from one generator seed, ties on 13 and
is behind on 11; it is not significantly different from ReduMIS, NuMVC or
FastVC. An ablation separates exact repair from reductions inside the region,
and measurements of the residual kernel show that its size does not by itself
predict where the method wins. More recent solvers (for example InfVC) were not
run, and the manuscript states this as a limitation.

PRIOR REVIEW

A previous version was reviewed by another journal and not accepted. The
reviews identified missing prior art, errors in the statistical comparison and
missing experiments; all are addressed in this version.

Every reported solution that a solver emits is independently verified; source
code, instance generators, checksums of every instance file and raw per-run
records are available at https://github.com/vinhqdang/max_independence_set.
The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
